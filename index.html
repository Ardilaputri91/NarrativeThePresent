2. Read the Story

Do not translate the story word by word. Focus on the main idea, characters, events, and evidence.

Paragraph 1

Jason is a teenage boy who spends most of his time inside his room, playing video games. His mother worries because he seems more comfortable in his virtual world than in the real world.

Paragraph 2

One day, Jason's mother comes home carrying a small cardboard box. She smiles and says that she has something special for him.

Paragraph 3

Inside the box is an energetic puppy. The puppy immediately tries to play with Jason and moves around excitedly.

Paragraph 4

Jason notices that the puppy has only three legs. He looks disappointed and does not seem interested in playing with it.

Paragraph 5

His mother explains that the puppy was born missing one leg. Jason listens for a moment, then turns back to his video game.

Paragraph 6

The puppy keeps trying to get Jason's attention. Jason ignores it and finally says, “Go away!” The puppy does not give up.

Paragraph 7

The puppy sees a ball and runs after it. Although it has only three legs, it moves confidently and happily.

Paragraph 8

The puppy falls while chasing the ball. It gets up and tries again. Jason watches the puppy and slowly becomes interested in what it is doing.

Paragraph 9

The puppy walks toward the door and looks back at Jason. Jason slowly gets up from his chair and follows the puppy.

Paragraph 10

Jason realizes that he and the puppy may not be so different. The puppy's physical limitation does not stop it from enjoying life. Jason begins to think that perhaps the bigger problem is the way he has looked at himself.

Paragraph 11

Jason goes outside with the puppy. For the first time in a long while, he leaves his virtual world and becomes active in the real world.

Paragraph 12

The puppy's greatest gift is not simply friendship. It gives Jason a new way of seeing himself and encourages him to face the real world with more confidence.
